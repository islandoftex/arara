arara is an Island of TeX project. The Island focuses on the
community aspect — bug reports, feature requests, questions about
our tools, and general discussions about TeX and its ecosystem are
always welcome. For us, tools are not about the cool tech, but
about how they help users. Come say hello in the
[community room](https://matrix.to/#/!titTeSvZiqNOvRIKCv:matrix.org?via=matrix.org),
or visit the [Island's home page](https://islandoftex.gitlab.io/).
