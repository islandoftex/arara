DevLab Theme `v0.1.2` is a small polish release for page navigation.

## Before

Blog posts did not show where they belonged, while documentation pages already had breadcrumbs. On mobile, documentation previous/next links also took too much space and stacked into large blocks.

## After

Breadcrumbs now use one shared template and work for both documentation pages and blog posts.

Documentation previous/next links are more compact on mobile. They stay readable, but no longer take over the bottom of the page.

Sites without a blog or without documentation still build normally.
